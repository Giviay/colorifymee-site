Frequently asked questions

How do I print a page?

Open the page and tap Print. You can also save it as a PDF or to your Photos, or share it to another app. Paper size and orientation are in the print settings.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google or your email (we send a 6-digit code, no password). Your account keeps your Plus subscription and free-page count with you on every device.

How do I cancel Colorlamma Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. In the app, open More → Colorlamma Plus → Manage, or go to your App Store / Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Where are my pages saved?

Your pages, artwork photos and child profiles are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so save or print anything you want to keep first.

How many pages can I make for free?

You get 2 free AI-made pages to try it out, including during a free trial. Colorlamma Plus gives you up to 50 AI-made pages a month (at most 20 a day). Ready-made pages in the Free store, coloring games and the Coloring Academy aren’t limited and don’t use up your AI pages.

A page didn’t turn out right. What can I do?

AI results vary. Try again, choose a different style, or describe the idea in more detail. Pages with very busy backgrounds work best with the Simple style.

How do I delete my data?

Open More → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account and everything on our servers, and clears the app on your device. You can also email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
