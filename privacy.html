Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Colourmee (“we”, “us”) makes an app that turns your photos and ideas into printable coloring pages, with ready-made pages, the Coloring Academy, coloring games and puzzles, flashcards and a Coloring Calendar. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Colourmee is designed to be used by parents and other adults.

1. Summary

  • You sign in with Apple, Google or your email address. We store only your email address or Apple / Google account ID. There’s never a password.
  • The photo, prompt or flashcard word you choose is sent to our AI provider only to make your page, and isn’t kept on our servers afterwards.
  • Your pages, artwork, child profiles, Academy and game progress, and your answers to the questions after sign-up stay on your device.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use third-party tracking.

2. Information we process

Photos and prompts you submit. When you create a page or a flashcard, or ask to change, redo or color in a page you made, the photo, text prompt or word you choose, along with your style settings (and the page itself, when you change it), is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to our AI image provider to generate the page. The app asks for your permission before anything is sent, and you can turn this off at any time in More → AI page creation. We don’t keep your photos or prompts on our servers after the page is created, and we don’t use them for advertising or to train our own models.

Your account. To use Colourmee you create an account with Apple, Google or your email address. We store your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address), your Apple or Google account ID and the name they share, plus counts of the AI pages you’ve created (in total, this month and today) and whether you’re in a free trial, so we can apply page limits and prevent abuse. Your account lets you use the app on other devices.

Purchases. Colourmee Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle payment. We don’t receive your card details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase and subscription status together with your account ID, so Plus works on all your devices.

Questions after sign-up. After you sign up, the app asks a few optional questions (for example your goals, how often you color and what your child loves) to suggest a plan. Every question can be skipped. Your answers are used only on your device to show that plan; they aren’t saved or sent to us or anyone else.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

3. What stays on your device

Your name (if you add one), coloring pages, flashcards, artwork photos, child profiles (names, avatars and photos), each child’s Coloring Academy age, learning path and progress, coloring game and puzzle progress and stickers, which children are playing, calendar plans and reminders, language and print settings are saved only on your device. We can’t see them. Deleting the app removes them.

4. How we use information

  • To create the coloring pages and flashcards you ask for.
  • To apply free-page limits and provide paid features.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.
  • To answer your support requests.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • OpenAI: generates coloring pages and flashcards from your photo, prompt or word. Under its API terms, data sent through the API isn’t used to train its models by default.
  • Supabase: hosts our server, database and storage, and handles sign-in (account and page counts).
  • RevenueCat: manages Colourmee Plus subscriptions (purchase history and subscription status, linked to your account ID).
  • Apple and Google: process in-app purchases and deliver the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users. We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos and prompts: not stored after your page is generated. Our AI provider may keep API data for a limited time (typically up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring, then delete it.
  • Account (ID, email, page count, Plus status): kept while you use the app, and deleted when you use Delete my data or ask us.

7. Device permissions

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to take or pick a photo. Saving to Photos is used only when you tap Save. Printing, saving PDFs to Files and sharing use your device’s own print and share options, and only send something where you choose. Notifications are used only for reminders you set in the Coloring Calendar; they are scheduled on your device. You can change these permissions in your device settings at any time.

8. Children

Colourmee is meant to be used by parents and other adults, not by children on their own. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Child profiles you create (including a child’s age for the Coloring Academy), their progress, and photos of your children’s artwork, stay on your device and aren’t shared with other users; the app has no public or social features. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything in More → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account and all server data (account and page counts) and clears the app’s data on your device.
  • Turn off sending photos and prompts to our AI provider in More → AI page creation.
  • Delete everything stored on your device by deleting the app.
  • Ask us to access or correct information linked to your account, or to delete it for you, by emailing us. We may ask you to confirm the email address or Apple / Google account you sign in with, so we know it’s you.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (making the pages you ask for) and our legitimate interests (keeping the service safe) as legal bases. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, sign-in uses one-time codes or your Apple / Google account (no passwords). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests: support@yourdomain.com
