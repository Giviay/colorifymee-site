Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Colorifymee (“we”, “us”) makes an app that turns your photos and ideas into printable coloring pages. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Colorifymee is designed to be used by parents and other adults.

1. Summary

  • Sign-in is optional: without it the app uses an anonymous account. If you sign in, we store only your email address or Apple / Google account ID. There’s never a password.
  • The photo or prompt you choose is sent to our AI provider only to make your coloring page, and isn’t kept on our servers afterwards.
  • Your pages, artwork and child profiles are stored on your device.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use third-party tracking.

2. Information we process

Photos and prompts you submit. When you create a page, the photo or text prompt you choose, along with your style settings, is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to our AI image provider to generate the page. The app asks for your permission before anything is sent, and you can turn this off at any time in More → AI page creation. We don’t keep your photos or prompts on our servers after the page is created, and we don’t use them for advertising or to train our own models.

Your account. We create an anonymous user ID for your installation and store a count of pages you’ve created, so we can apply free-page limits and prevent abuse. If you choose to sign in with Apple, Google or email, we also store your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address), your Apple or Google account ID, and the name they share, so you can use the app on other devices.

Community (family circles). If you create or join a family circle, we store the display name you choose, the artwork photos and captions you share, likes, comments, reports, blocks and circle activity notifications, so other members of that circle can see them. Only members of a circle can see its content.

Purchases. Colorifymee Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle payment. We don’t receive your card details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase and subscription status together with your account ID, so Plus works on all your devices.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

3. What stays on your device

Your name (if you add one), coloring pages, artwork photos, child profiles (names and avatars), calendar plans and reminders, language and print settings are saved only on your device. We can’t see them. Deleting the app removes them.

4. How we use information

  • To create the coloring pages you ask for.
  • To run family circles you choose to join.
  • To apply free-page limits and provide paid features.
  • To keep the service safe, including reviewing reported content and preventing misuse.
  • To answer your support requests.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • OpenAI: generates coloring pages from your photo or prompt. Under its API terms, data sent through the API isn’t used to train its models by default.
  • Supabase: hosts our server, database and storage, and handles sign-in (account, page counts and family circle content).
  • RevenueCat: manages Colorifymee Plus subscriptions (purchase history and subscription status, linked to your account ID).
  • Apple and Google: process in-app purchases and deliver the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users. We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos and prompts: not stored after your page is generated. Our AI provider may keep API data for a limited time (typically up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring, then delete it.
  • Family circle content: kept until you delete it, delete the circle, use Delete my data, or ask us to delete it.
  • Reports and blocks: kept while needed to review them and keep circles safe, and deleted with your account.
  • Account (ID, email, page count, Plus status): kept while you use the app, and deleted when you use Delete my data or ask us.

7. Device permissions

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to take or pick a photo. Saving to Photos is used only when you tap Save. Notifications are used only for reminders you set in the Coloring Calendar; they are scheduled on your device. You can change these permissions in your device settings at any time.

8. Children

Colorifymee is meant to be used by parents and other adults, not by children on their own. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Child profiles you create stay on your device. Please don’t share photos of children in family circles unless you’re their parent or guardian and are comfortable with circle members seeing them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your own posts and comments, leave a family circle, report posts and comments, or block people, at any time in the app.
  • Delete everything in More → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account and all server data (circles you own, posts, comments, likes, reports and page counts) and clears the app’s data on your device.
  • Turn off sending photos and prompts to our AI provider in More → AI page creation.
  • Delete everything stored on your device by deleting the app.
  • Ask us to access or correct information linked to your account, or to delete it for you, by emailing us. Because there’s no login, we may ask for details that help us find your data (for example, the name of a circle you joined).

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (making the pages you ask for and running circles you join) and our legitimate interests (keeping the service safe) as legal bases. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, sign-in uses one-time codes or your Apple / Google account (no passwords), and access to family circle content is restricted to circle members. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests: support@yourdomain.com
